Mental model: each executing frame is a clerk in a business's office — the bytecode is the wall, the stack is a pile of slips, memory is the desk, storage is the filing cabinet, gas is a stamp budget. CALL dispatches a sub-request to another business — a new clerk, same country. Every world-state write files an undo receipt; a failed call tears up its receipts.
